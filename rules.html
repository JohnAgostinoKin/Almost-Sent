rules.

the almost sent weekly contest, in full. no purchase necessary to enter or win.

eligibility

open to individuals 18 years of age or older. void where prohibited by law. by entering, you represent that you meet these requirements.

how to enter

submit a line through the contest box on almostsent.app, under a result you didn't laugh at. one line per entry.

judging

entries are reviewed and selected by almost sent. one winning entry is chosen approximately once a week, at almost sent's sole discretion, based on which line is funniest.

prize

$50 (USD), awarded to the selected weekly winner approximately once per week that a winner is selected. arrangements for delivering the prize are coordinated directly with the winner. actual value may vary; no cash-equivalent substitution except at almost sent's discretion.

notification

if you provide an email address at entry, we may use it to notify you if you win. if you don't, we may not be able to reach you, and an unclaimed prize may be forfeited or awarded to another entrant.

general

almost sent may modify or cancel this contest at any time. almost sent is not responsible for entries lost, delayed, or misdirected. sponsor: almost sent (almostsent.app).
