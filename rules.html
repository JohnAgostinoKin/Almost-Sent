rules.

the almost sent weekly contest, in full. no purchase necessary to enter or win.

eligibility

open to individuals 18 years of age or older who reside in the united states. void where prohibited by law. by entering, you represent that you meet these requirements.

contest period

each weekly period runs monday 12:00 a.m. to sunday 11:59 p.m. eastern time, beginning monday, september 14, 2026. one winner is selected from each period.

how to enter

submit a line through the contest box on almostsent.app, under a result you didn't laugh at. one line per entry.

entry limit

unlimited entries per person; one prize per person per period. employees of the sponsor and their immediate family are not eligible.

judging

entries are reviewed and selected by almost sent. one winning entry is chosen from each weekly period, at almost sent's sole discretion, based on which line is funniest.

prize

one (1) $50 Amazon.com gift card, approximate retail value $50 USD, delivered by email to the address provided at entry within 7 days of selection. the winner must reply to confirm within 14 days or the prize is forfeited and may be awarded to another entrant. the gift card is subject to amazon's terms, is not transferable, and is not redeemable for cash. the winner is responsible for any taxes.

notification

if you provide an email address at entry, we will email you if you win. if you don't, we won't be able to reach you, and the prize may be forfeited or awarded to another entrant.

license

by entering, you grant almost sent a non-exclusive, perpetual license to display and use your submitted line, anonymously, including to improve the app's generated responses. your line is shown publicly on the wall only if you checked the "show my line on the wall" box at the time of entry.

retention

the 30-day deletion window described in our privacy policy does not apply to contest entries. your line, the text it answered, and any email you provided are retained beyond that window for contest administration and the license above. contact info@almostsent.app to request deletion.

winners list

to request the name of a weekly winner, email info@almostsent.app within 30 days of the end of that period.

general

almost sent may modify or cancel this contest at any time. almost sent is not responsible for entries lost, delayed, or misdirected. sponsor: Bonzoli Technology Group, Inc., PO Box 122, Clemson, SC 29633.
