privacy.

almost sent is a joke generator. Here's exactly what happens to what you paste.

what's kept

The line you paste is sent to an AI provider to write the draft, and a copy is stored so we can improve future drafts. Before it's stored, anything that looks like a phone number or an email address is replaced with [phone] or [email] — that scrubbing is best-effort, not a guarantee, so still don't paste someone's full name, number, or other identifying details.

We don't keep whatever you type into the "think you can do better" box, and we don't keep your name — the app never asks for one.

for how long

30 days. A nightly job deletes anything older than that automatically.

training

we don't use your text to train anything. it's sent to a third-party AI provider to generate the result, under their policies.

anonymous usage

The app also logs anonymous interaction events — a random id generated in your browser, which button you tapped, and small structured details like which joke "shape" it was. Never the text of what you pasted or wrote, and never anything tied to your identity.

age

18+. The jokes are crude on purpose.
